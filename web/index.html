AWAITING DATASET
01

Computer vision

Image reports include amplified recompression residuals and optional reference differences. Video reports include 24 sampled frames, face-region boxes, geometric track IDs, and short consecutive-frame windows. Motion, compression, lighting, and ordinary editing can produce unusual measurements.

02

Machine learning

Image prediction uses a pinned original Community Forensics checkpoint, converted locally and numerically checked. It compares real photographs with AI-generated imagery across three input versions and abstains when scores are ambiguous. It does not detect every Photoshop edit or misleading context. Videos retain the separate experimental face-image detector. Model scores are uncalibrated; benchmark measurements have a limited dataset scope.

03

Claims and sources

English-language rules extract claim candidates and highlight loaded phrases. Optional online lookup uses recent public fact-check titles, or Google Fact Check Tools when configured. Retrieved ratings describe the source’s reviewed claim; matching it to this article requires human review.

04

Data and provenance

Each account has a separate history. Media and article text are held in temporary worker folders and removed after processing. Reports retain visual excerpts and extracted claims. Embedded C2PA credentials are verified locally against a bundled trust-anchor snapshot; valid credentials do not prove factual truth.
